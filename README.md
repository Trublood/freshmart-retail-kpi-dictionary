FreshMart Retail KPI DictionaryProject OverviewThis project creates a business-led Retail KPI Dictionary for FreshMart, a fictional UK supermarket chain. The dictionary acts as a single business reference for the core performance measures used across a Retail Analytics Data Warehouse.The project focuses on business definitions and KPI governance, not technical implementation. It establishes 36 KPIs across eight areas: Sales \& Trading, Customer, Store Performance, Inventory \& Availability, Promotions, Operations \& Labour, Supply Chain, and Finance \& Profitability.Business ProblemRetail teams can use the same KPI name while applying different definitions or calculation rules. For example, sales can differ depending on the treatment of VAT, discounts, refunds and returns; customer transactions can also be incorrectly treated as unique customers.Without agreed definitions, different reports can produce conflicting results even when the underlying systems are working correctly.ObjectiveCreate a concise, controlled business reference that helps analysts, developers and all stakeholders in the company use consistent KPI definitions across FreshMart reporting.Deliverables•	Executive Summary•	Project Brief and Business Understanding•	36 core retail KPI definitions•	KPI category framework•	KPI standardisation and governance rules•	Glossary of retail termsKPI CategoriesCategory	KPIsSales \& Trading	6Customer	5Store Performance	4Inventory \& Availability	6Promotions	4Operations \& Labour	4Supply Chain	3Finance \& Profitability	4Total	36Approach1\.	Defined the business problem, scope and intended users.2\.	Grouped reporting needs into eight retail KPI categories.3\.	Selected 36 decision-useful KPIs.4\.	Defined the business purpose, definition, formula and calculation rules for each KPI.5\.	Reviewed overlapping and dependent metrics to remove ambiguity.6\.	Established common standards for sales, customers, inventory, promotions, labour and finance.7\.	Assigned primary business ownership and change-control principles.Key Business Rules•	Net Sales is the standard sales base for dependent KPIs unless stated otherwise.•	Customer Transactions and Unique Customers are separate measures.•	Stock Availability % and Out-of-Stock Rate use the same availability framework.•	Waste and Shrinkage are different.•	Sales per Trading Hour and Sales per Labour Hour measure different types of productivity.•	Promotional success is not assessed using sales uplift alone; profitability must also be considered.•	Like-for-Like reporting follows separately governed store eligibility rules.Business ValueThe dictionary supports consistent management reporting, clearer requirements for analytics development, reduced metric duplication, improved trust in reporting and stronger KPI governance.Tools / Skills Demonstrated•	Retail business analysis•	KPI design and standardisation•	Requirements definition•	Metric governance•	Retail commercial understanding•	Data warehouse business requirements•	Documentation and stakeholder communicationRepository Files•	README.md - project overview•	FreshMart\_Retail\_KPI\_Dictionary.pdf - final portfolio document•	FreshMart\_Retail\_KPI\_Dictionary.docx - editable version of the portfolio documentNoteFreshMart is a fictional company created for portfolio purposes.
